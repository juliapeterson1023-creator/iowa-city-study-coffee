Meet Common Table Coffee

Imagine a welcoming café with shared tables, movable chairs, and a lively hum of conversation. Common Table Coffee is designed around small student groups that need space for laptops, notebooks, and discussion.

In this imagined Iowa City café, tables seat four to six people. A separate collaboration area keeps group discussions together, while a front lounge offers smaller tables for partners reviewing an assignment.

What makes a space work for groups?

Tables with room to share

Large surfaces give teammates space to open laptops, compare notes, and review project materials together.

Conversation-friendly seating

A dedicated collaboration area welcomes discussion at a reasonable volume without the expectations of a silent room.

Flexible arrangements

Movable chairs help groups face one another, share a screen, and include every teammate in the conversation.

Choose a task that benefits from teamwork

  • Brainstorm ideas and divide responsibilities for a project.
  • Compare class notes and explain difficult concepts.
  • Review presentation slides and practice transitions quietly.
  • Give feedback on outlines, examples, and supporting evidence.

Make your group study time count

  1. Agree on one outcome before meeting, such as finishing an outline or reviewing a practice set.
  2. Bring the assignment instructions and the materials everyone needs to participate.
  3. Give each person a chance to contribute, and keep a shared list of decisions.
  4. Finish by assigning next steps and confirming when each task is due.

Share the café thoughtfully

Our imagined café welcomes collaboration while asking groups to keep voices at a conversational level. Headphones are encouraged for audio, bags stay out of walkways, and chairs return to their original places after a meeting.
