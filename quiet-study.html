Some assignments call for a little extra quiet. Our fictional café concept, Margin & Mug, imagines a space for students who want to read, write, and work independently with fewer distractions.

Meet Margin & Mug

Imagine warm wood desks, soft green walls, and a reading room where conversations stay outside. Margin & Mug combines the comfortable feel of a coffee shop with clear expectations for quiet independent work.

In this imagined Iowa City café, drinks are ordered in a separate front area. Students can then choose an individual desk in the quiet room or a window seat for shorter reading sessions. Calls and group discussions belong in the front lounge, away from the study area.

A space designed for independent study

Individual desks

One-person workspaces provide room for a laptop, notebook, and drink without needing to share a large group table.

Power within reach

Each imagined desk includes access to a power outlet, making it easier to keep a laptop charged during a longer assignment.

A conversation-free room

Silent phones, headphones, and a separate ordering area help maintain a quieter setting for reading and writing.

Which assignments fit this setting?

A quiet café can suit tasks that require sustained individual attention. In our fictional guide, Margin & Mug is the choice for:

  • Reading course chapters and taking notes.
  • Outlining essays or revising a first draft.
  • Reviewing flashcards with headphones on.
  • Working through practice problems independently.

If your assignment involves discussing ideas or rehearsing a presentation aloud, explore our group study café for a setting designed around conversation.

Try a focused study plan

  1. Choose one clear goal, such as outlining your introduction or reviewing one chapter.
  2. Set out only the materials you need and silence unnecessary notifications.
  3. Work for a manageable block of time, then take a short break away from your screen.
  4. Review what you finished and choose your next task.

Keep the quiet space comfortable for everyone

In our imagined café, students keep calls outside the reading room, use headphones at a low volume, and keep bags out of walkways. During busy periods, a two-hour seating guideline gives more students a chance to use a desk.
