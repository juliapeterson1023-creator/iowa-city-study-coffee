Meet Penny & Pour

Penny & Pour imagines a casual student café with a short menu, clearly displayed prices, and comfortable seating. Its concept centers on simple drinks that make planning your spending straightforward.

The fictional space includes small tables for independent work and a shared counter for shorter visits. Students can choose a basic brewed coffee or spend a little more on an espresso drink.

What could a $10 weekly budget cover?

Using this invented menu, a $10 drink budget could cover four drip coffees at $2.50 each. Another option would be one $4.00 latte and two $2.50 drip coffees, totaling $9.00 before any additional costs.

Buying three lattes would cost $12.00. Switching one of those lattes to drip coffee would bring the total to $10.50. Switching two would bring it to $9.00.

These examples show how drink choice and visit frequency affect a budget. Choose an amount that fits your own spending priorities.

Build a simple coffee spending plan

  1. Decide how much you want to spend on café drinks each week.
  2. Estimate how many café study sessions you expect to have.
  3. Multiply the price of each drink by the number you plan to buy, allowing room for additional costs.
  4. Track your purchases and adjust your choices or visit frequency if you exceed your plan.

Consider the study space as well as the price

A lower drink price is only one part of choosing a study setting. Think about your task, the noise level, available seating, and whether you need to talk with teammates.
